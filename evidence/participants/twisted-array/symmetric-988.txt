# Structure. sigma is the map (x1, x2) -> (x1, -x2) of G, with sigma applied twice the identity, and tau is (x1, x2) -> (-x1, x2), which is sigma followed by negation. H is the group of order 9 generated by (x1, x2) -> (3 x1, 4 x2). On the integers modulo 247, sigma is multiplication by 170 and the generator of H is multiplication by 42.
# Unknowns. Three functions a, b, d from G to {1, -1}: a constant on the orbits of the group generated by H and tau (17 orbits), b constant on the orbits of H (31 orbits), d constant on the orbits of the group generated by H and sigma (18 orbits). The fourth function is c(x) = b(sigma x).
# Equation. PAF_a(s) + PAF_b(s) + PAF_c(s) + PAF_d(s) = 0 at every nonzero s in G, where PAF_f(s) is the sum of f(x) f(x + s) over x in G.
