vxvware

Complete vxvware censuses past the range classified by Đoković and Kotsireas [8]: four classes at n=51, three at n=55, none at n=57, and eight quadruples at n=59 that the source counts as two classes and equiv-v1 as one. Existence at 51 and 55 was already known from Spence's infinite family [12] (q = 101 and 109); the class counts there and the existence at 59 are new. Verified rows give existence and lower bounds; the empty order is an unreplayed attributed claim.

Original source attribution: vxvware

The four canonical rows, one row of cells per block (A k · B s · C s · D k; k skew, s symmetric), and the Goethals–Seidel array they generate: a Hadamard matrix of order 204. Skew blocks are green, symmetric blocks blue and blocks with neither symmetry grey; light is +1 and dark is −1. The drawing is computed from the rows in the browser; the verifier result below is the evidence.
