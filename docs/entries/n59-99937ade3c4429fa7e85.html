Complete vxvware censuses past the range classified in arXiv:1802.00556: four classes at n=51, three at n=55, none at n=57, and eight quadruples at n=59 that the source counts as two classes and equiv-v1 as one. Verified rows give existence and lower bounds; the empty order is an unreplayed attributed claim.

The four canonical rows, one row of cells per block (A s · B k · C k · D s; k skew, s symmetric), and the Goethals–Seidel array they generate: a Hadamard matrix of order 236.
