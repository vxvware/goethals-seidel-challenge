One quadruple with a skew block at n=81 over the cyclic group Z_81, found on 2026-11-21 by a search that fixes the skew block and the 3-compressions of the other three blocks, enumerates every lift of each compressed block, keeps the lifts whose periodic autocorrelations lie in five-value windows, and joins the three pools exactly. The search code is not in the repository; the run record is evidence/participants/compression/skew-81.txt. A skew-Hadamard matrix of order 324 has been known since 1994, from supplementary difference sets in the elementary abelian group of order 81 [6]; the order is not new and is not among the open orders of baselines/skew-gaps.json. The board's entry is over the cyclic group. A search of current sources on 2026-11-21 found no earlier quadruple with a skew block over Z_81; that is a report of the search, not a proof of priority, and no first existence is claimed. This is the board's first quadruple with a skew block at n=81. The join stopped at its first solution, so the search is a sample and not a census.
