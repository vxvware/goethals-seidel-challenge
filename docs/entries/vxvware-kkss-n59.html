Complete vxvware censuses past the range classified by Đoković and Kotsireas [10]: four classes at n=51, three at n=55, none at n=57, and eight quadruples at n=59 that the source counts as two classes and equiv-v1 as one. Existence at 51 and 55 was already known from Spence's infinite family [19] (q = 101 and 109); the class counts at 51 and 55, the nonexistence at 57, and the existence and count at 59 are new. Replays with pinned engines between 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 returned the same classes at each order, so the four counts are exact. The empty cell at 53 is not a search result: a (kkss) parameter set exists only when 2v - 1 is a sum of two squares, by the parameter-set condition of [10], and 105 is not.
