A search with the two skew rows constant on the orbits of the subgroup generated by 4 reproduces the known Spence example at parameters (63; 31,31,27,25; 51). It is not a census and establishes no count.

Original source attribution: vxvware
Submitted / imported by: vxvware · 2026-10-15

Picture

The four canonical rows, one row of cells per block (A s · B k · C s · D k; k skew, s symmetric), and the Goethals–Seidel array they generate: a Hadamard matrix of order 252. Skew blocks are green and symmetric blocks blue; dark is +1 and light is −1. The drawing is computed from the rows in the browser; the verifier result below is the evidence.
