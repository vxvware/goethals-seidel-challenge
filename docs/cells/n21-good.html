The Williamson entries include every class of Holzmann, Kharaghani and Tayfeh-Rezaie [10] through order 59, and both classes at 63 and the Turyn class [14] at 69 from the MathCheck enumeration of Bright, Kotsireas and Ganesh [1] [4].

Each figure shows the four canonical rows and the Goethals–Seidel array they generate, a Hadamard matrix of order 84. Skew blocks are green, symmetric blocks blue and blocks with neither symmetry grey; light is +1 and dark is −1.
