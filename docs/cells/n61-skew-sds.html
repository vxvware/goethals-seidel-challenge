The exhaustive vxvware gmat_c census at n=61, the first open G-matrix order, found a (kkss) quadruple with row sums (1, 1, -11, -11) in its ninth piece on 2026-10-18 and finished all 32 pieces on 2026-10-22 after 811 cpu-hours: twelve quadruples, two equiv-v1 classes (three under the source's own convention, which does not transpose blocks). Spence's family [19] does not reach 61, Đoković and Kotsireas [10] classify through 49, and the 2009 table of cyclic families in [9] leaves type (kkss) unresolved at (61; 30, 30, 25, 25; 49), the only parameter set of that type at 61. The rows verify exactly; the count of two became exact on 2026-11-15, when a replay with the pinned kkss-census-c-v3 engine returned the same classes. The first quadruple keeps its hand-entered id and the census maps its class onto it.
