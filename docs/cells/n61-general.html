Challenge resultNot yet published

vxvware

The exhaustive vxvware gmat_c census at n=61, the first open G-matrix order, found a (kkss) quadruple with row sums (1, 1, -11, -11) in its ninth piece on 2026-10-18 and finished all 32 pieces on 2026-10-22 after 811 cpu-hours: twelve quadruples, two equiv-v1 classes (three under the source's own convention, which does not transpose blocks). Spence's family [12] does not reach 61, Đoković and Kotsireas [8] classify through 49, and Djokovic (2024) left the parameter set (61; 24, 28, 28, 28; 47) open. The rows verify exactly; the count of two became exact on 2026-11-15, when a replay with the pinned kkss-census-c-v3 engine returned the same classes. The first quadruple keeps its hand-entered id and the census maps its class onto it.

vxvware

The vxvware census found good matrices at three orders no published search covers: 104 rows at n=53, 40 at n=55, and 120 at n=61, each yielding one equiv-v1 class. The rows verify exactly; replays with the pinned good-census-c-v1 engine on 2026-11-14 to 2026-11-16 returned the same classes, so the three counts are exact.
