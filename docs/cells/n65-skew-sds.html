One quadruple with a skew block at each of n=65, 67, 69 and 71, found on 2026-11-19 by the sampling search in research/starter/skew: the skew block is fixed in advance, invariant under a multiplier subgroup of odd order, and the other three blocks are sampled inside per-lag autocorrelation windows and joined exactly. The skew-Hadamard orders 260, 268, 276 and 284 are not among the open orders of baselines/skew-gaps.json; these are the board's first quadruples with a skew block at those n, without a claim of first existence. Quadruples with a skew block over the cyclic group were already published at all four orders: constructions of 1992 at 65 and 67 are recounted in [6], 69 is in [11], and 71 belongs to the family of [22] for prime powers q = 4n - 1 (q = 283). The search is not a census and says nothing about how many classes exist.
