Xia, Xia, Seberry and Wu (2005) [22]; Paley (1933) [18]

One quadruple with a skew block at each of n=83 and 125, in the form A skew, B symmetric, C = D that [22] proves to exist for every prime power q = 4n - 1 (q = 331 and 499). The rows were generated on 2026-11-21 from the Paley matrices [18] of orders 332 and 500 by the script recorded in evidence/literature/paley-four-circulant.txt; they were not transcribed from the source. These are literature constructions, not new results and not search results, and the credit is the source's. The board had no quadruple with a skew block at those n before.
