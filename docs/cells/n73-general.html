vxvware

The vxvware sweep generated 15,360 quadruples, yielding eight verified equiv-v1 classes. Published skew-Hadamard existence at order 292, by a construction of Đoković recorded by Cati and Pasechnik [5], is credited separately; these are participant-generated families, without a claim of first existence at this order.

Imported reference constructions retain the original source attribution. Inclusion in the challenge catalogue does not make the importer their discoverer. The Williamson entries include every class of Holzmann, Kharaghani and Tayfeh-Rezaie [10] through order 59, and both classes at 63 and the Turyn class [14] at 69 from the MathCheck enumeration of Bright, Kotsireas and Ganesh [1] [4].
