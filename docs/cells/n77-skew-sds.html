One quadruple with a skew block at each of n=77, 103, 105, 109, 111, 117, 121 and 123, found on 2026-11-20 by the pool join of research/starter/skew restricted to quadruples in which all four blocks are constant on the orbits of a multiplier subgroup: order 3 at 77, 103, 105, 109, 111 and 117, order 5 at 121 and 123. The skew block and the row sums are fixed by the plan; the runs at 77 and 109 found one solution each, the run at 105 two, the others between 7 and 86. The skew-Hadamard orders 308, 412, 420, 436, 444, 468, 484 and 492 are not among the open orders of baselines/skew-gaps.json; these are the board's first quadruples with a skew block at those n, without a claim of first existence. Quadruples with a skew block over the cyclic group were already published at all eight orders: 77, 105, 111, 117 and 123 belong to the family of [22] for prime powers q = 4n - 1 (q = 307, 419, 443, 467 and 491), 103 is in [6], which also recounts a construction of 1992 at 121, and 109 is in [8]. The searches are samples of one orbit space each and are not a census of the cells.
