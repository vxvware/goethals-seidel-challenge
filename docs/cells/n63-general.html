n = 63 · General (any)

exists · verified-on-board · reproduced

vxvware

A search with the two skew rows constant on the orbits of the subgroup generated by 4 reproduces the known Spence example [12] at parameters (63; 31,31,27,25; 51). It is not a census and establishes no count.

Literature / catalogPublished result

Holzmann, Kharaghani and Tayfeh-Rezaie (2008) [10]; Turyn (1972) [14]; Bright, Kotsireas and Ganesh (2018) [1]; Bright, Kotsireas and Ganesh (2020) [4]

Imported reference constructions retain the original source attribution. Inclusion in the challenge catalogue does not make the importer their discoverer. The Williamson entries include every class of Holzmann, Kharaghani and Tayfeh-Rezaie [10] through order 59, and both classes at 63 and the Turyn class [14] at 69 from the MathCheck enumeration of Bright, Kotsireas and Ganesh [1] [4].

4 verified classes

Each figure shows the four canonical rows and the Goethals–Seidel array they generate, a Hadamard matrix of order 252. Skew blocks are green, symmetric blocks blue and blocks with neither symmetry grey; light is +1 and dark is −1.
