A search with the two skew rows constant on the orbits of the subgroup generated by 4 reproduces the known Spence example [12] at parameters (63; 31,31,27,25; 51). It is not a census and establishes no count.

2 verified classes

Each figure shows the four canonical rows and the Goethals–Seidel array they generate, a Hadamard matrix of order 252. Skew blocks are green, symmetric blocks blue and blocks with neither symmetry grey; light is +1 and dark is −1.
